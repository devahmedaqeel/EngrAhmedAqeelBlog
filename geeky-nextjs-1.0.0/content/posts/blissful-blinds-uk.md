**Blissful Blinds UK** is a full-featured commercial website engineered for a UK-based business client, complete with custom quote forms, automated business email, customer reviews, chatbot integration, and technical SEO optimizations.

### Tech Stack & Technologies

- **Frontend & Web:** React.js, JavaScript, CSS3
- **Backend Services:** Node.js, Nodemailer (Hostinger Email)
- **Deployment & SEO:** Vercel Hosting, Performance & On-Page SEO Optimization

### Key Features

- **Automated Quotation System:** Custom quotation forms that process customer measurements and send structured lead emails directly to the sales team.
- **Interactive Chatbot & Reviews:** Built-in customer review module and chatbot widget to enhance conversion rates.
- **SEO & Performance Optimization:** Optimized for fast page load speed, mobile responsiveness, and high Google search visibility.
